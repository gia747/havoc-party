Player Disclaimer

Please read before you play.

Photosensitivity warning: a small percentage of people may experience seizures when exposed to flashing lights, patterns, or rapidly changing imagery in video games | even with no prior history of epilepsy.

Havoc Party: Knockout Arena contains bright flashes, explosion effects, screen shake, and fast-moving lights, especially during eliminations, volcano hazards, and crowd celebrations.

Stop playing and consult a doctor if you experience

  • Dizziness, blurred vision, or eye/muscle twitching
  • Disorientation, confusion, or loss of awareness
  • Any involuntary movement or convulsion

Play safer

  • Play in a well-lit room and sit as far from the screen as practical.
  • Use a smaller screen rather than a large one if available.
  • Take a break every 30–60 minutes; do not play when tired or sleep-deprived.
  • Lower screen brightness if effects feel intense.

Parents should supervise children and stop play immediately if any symptoms appear.
