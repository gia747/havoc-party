Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains what data Havoc Party: Knockout Arena ("the Game"), by SheGoz Technologies, collects and why.

1. What We Collect

  • Player name — the display name you choose for multiplayer rooms.
  • Gameplay data — coins, owned outfits, battle-pass progress, match results, and friend lists, stored on our backend to sync your progress.
  • Technical data — device/OS version, crash information, and connection diagnostics needed to keep multiplayer working.
  • Verification token — a Cloudflare Turnstile token used to confirm you are a real player. It is sent to our server, verified once with Cloudflare, and exchanged for a short-lived ticket. We do not receive personal details from Cloudflare.

2. What We Do NOT Collect

  • No real name, email, phone number, or contacts.
  • No precise location.
  • No advertising identifiers, ad trackers, or third-party analytics profiles.
  • No microphone or camera data.

3. How Data Is Used

  • Matchmaking and multiplayer rooms.
  • Persisting coins, outfits, friends, and battle-pass progress.
  • Preventing abuse, bots, and attacks on the service.

4. Third-Party Processors

  • Cloudflare Turnstile — human-verification widget; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Railway — hosting for the multiplayer/economy backend.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting for this site.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Profile and economy data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion of your gameplay profile by contacting us through our official support channel; local progress on your device can be removed by uninstalling the Game.

6. Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The only stored data is the gameplay profile described above plus a self-chosen display name.

7. Security

Traffic to our servers uses TLS. Game builds ship with integrity and anti-tamper measures. No method is perfect, but we take reasonable steps to protect the service.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always posted here.
